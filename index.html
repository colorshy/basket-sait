01 // INDUSTRIAL DESIGN

RE-ENGINEERED
FROM THE CARCASS OUT.

Traditional basketballs are disposable by design. When exterior leather panels wear smooth or lose tactility, the entire carcass is discarded.

RE:FORM decouples the pneumatic pressure vessel from the tactile exterior. An autonomous high-rebound core receives replaceable leather shells through precision magnetic channel retention.

PANEL RETENTION Self-Aligning Magnetic Channels
